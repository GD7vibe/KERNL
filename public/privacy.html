Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

KERNL (kernlbooks.com) provides AI-written summaries of books that you can read and listen to. This page explains what personal data we collect, why, and who else handles it. We try to collect as little as possible.

Who we are

KERNL is run by Gary Duff, Plymouth, United Kingdom. For anything in this policy, email duffgaryduff@hotmail.com.

What we collect

  • Guests: if you use KERNL without signing in, we create an anonymous session with a random ID. It holds no name or email. We record which books you open so we can apply the free-books allowance.
  • Accounts: your email address and a password (stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider), or, if you use Google sign-in, the name and email address Google shares with us.
  • Your library and usage: the books you have opened or saved, how many summaries you have used, your plan (Free or Pro), and, once you have enough books, a short reading portrait and recommendations that we generate from the titles in your library.
  • Send to Kindle: the Kindle email address you type in, used once to send the file. We do not store it.
  • Technical data: IP address and basic request logs, used for security, abuse prevention and rate limiting, and anonymous page-view statistics.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Why we use it (legal basis)

  • To provide the service you asked for, including your account, library and allowance (contract).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (legitimate interests).
  • To send you account emails such as confirmations and password resets (contract).

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage.
  • Vercel — hosting and anonymous page-view analytics.
  • Anthropic — generates summaries and reading portraits. We send book titles and authors, not your name or email.
  • xAI — turns summary text into audio. We send only the summary text.
  • Resend — delivers email, including Send to Kindle.
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google.

Some of these providers are based outside the UK. Where personal data is transferred, they rely on recognised safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and library until you ask us to delete them. Anonymous guest sessions and their book records may be deleted if they have been inactive for a long time.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and object to how we use it. Email the address above and we will respond within one month. If you are unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and remember settings such as dark mode and recently opened summaries. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top.
