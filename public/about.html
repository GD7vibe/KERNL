The point of a book,without the hours.

KERNL is for people who like the idea of books but have no time, or no inclination, to read them. You get the argument in ten minutes. You read it, or you listen to it.

Life is short. The shelf is long.

There are millions of books and one of you. A typical non-fiction book takes four to six hours to read, and most of that is repetition, padding and stories told so that a single point sticks.

If you love reading for its own sake, keep your books. If you have ever bought one, got forty pages in and quietly given up, this is for you.

The 80/20 rule

The 80/20 rule is a rule of thumb, not a law: roughly 80% of the result comes from 20% of the effort. Books follow it closely. A handful of ideas carry most of the value, and the rest is there to make them memorable, or to fill the page count.

KERNL aims at that 20%.

20%of the pages. The ideas you will actually use and remember.

What you get

  • About 1,500 words. The central argument, the key ideas, and what to do with them.
  • A voice to read it to you. Press play in the car, on a walk, or while you cook.
  • Kindle and print. Send it to your Kindle, or print a one-pager with a link to buy the book.
  • Mega Words. The interesting words from each book, in plain English.
An honest note. KERNL summaries are written by AI from its knowledge of each book. They give you the argument, not the experience, and they can contain mistakes, so check anything that matters. If a book grabs you, read it and support the author.
